# 🌿 Adëeva Product & SKU Catalog

> **Local Product Database**: You can add, edit, or delete products directly in the table below.  
> Whenever you add a product name and SKU number here, it will automatically appear in the **Section 4 Product Dropdown** in the app, and selecting the product will auto-select its SKU number!

---

### 📝 How to Add a New Product
Simply add a new line at the bottom of the table:
```markdown
| Your Product Name | SKU-CODE |
```
