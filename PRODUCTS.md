---

### 📝 How to Add a New Product
Simply add a new line at the bottom of the table below:
```markdown
| Your Product Name | SKU-CODE | 29.95 | 60 capsules |
```
- **Product Name**: The full name displayed in the dropdown.
- **SKU**: The exact SKU / catalog number (e.g. `KNS-000015`).
- **Price**: Unit price in CAD (numbers only, e.g. `24.50`).
- **Description**: Packaging or dosage info (e.g. `90 softgels`).
